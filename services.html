What We Offer

Full Refinishing

A full refinish includes sanding down to the bare wood to get any deep imperfections out of the floor and either finishing the floor with a natural finish or applying stain followed by a premium polyurethane finish.

Buff and Coat

A buff and coat is for when you have top scratches or small imperfections in the top coat of your floor finish. We lightly abrade your floors and then top it off with a fresh finish coat.

Board Replacement

If there are any broken and or rotted baords we can rip out and lace in new matching hardwood to match your existing floor and floor pattern.

We provide full refinsishes where we sand down to the bare floor and either stain or give your floors a natural finish. If your floors just have those pesky top scratches a buff and coat may be a better option for you.
